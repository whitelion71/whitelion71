# Waed Track (مسار واعد) — PRIMARY

**Model:** The sponsoring national entity nominates or selects the candidate, the Ministry funds the scholarship, and the scholar joins that entity after graduating.

## Requirements (VERIFY for the current cycle)
- [ ] Eligibility (age, degree, GPA minimum): VERIFY on the official MoE scholarship portal
- [ ] Entity-specific application window: VERIFY per entity
- [ ] Is an unconditional admission required before nomination, or only after? VERIFY
- [ ] Approved universities / majors list per entity: VERIFY

## Process (typical, confirm per entity)
1. Monitor entity career/scholarship pages and LinkedIn for Waed announcements.
2. Apply to the entity (CV, transcripts, sometimes tests and interviews).
3. Entity selection, then a nomination letter.
4. Secure an admission at an approved university and program.
5. Register on the scholarship portal, then sign the scholarship and employment contract.
