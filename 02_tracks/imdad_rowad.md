# Imdad (إمداد) & Al-Rowad (الرواد) Tracks — PARALLEL SAFETY

| | Al-Rowad (Pioneers) | Imdad (Supply) |
|---|---|---|
| University tier | Top-ranked global universities (approved list) | Wider approved ranking band, priority majors |
| Major restriction | Broad | Tied to labor-market priority majors |
| Key gate | **Unconditional offer** | **Unconditional offer** + approved major |

> All ranking thresholds, approved-university lists and major lists change each cycle. Mark `VERIFY` until confirmed from the official source.

## Strategy
- Apply to **2–3 reach** schools (Al-Rowad tier), **4–5 target** schools (Imdad tier) and **1–2 safety** schools.
- Choose programs where the **US-degree English waiver** applies, so the offer is unconditional from day one.
- Confirm that each program title maps to a priority major on the approved list.
