# Positioning & Narrative

## Core Line
> A Telecommunications & Digital Infrastructure Engineer transitioning into securing and automating next-generation networks using Artificial Intelligence.

## Story Pillars
1. **Foundation:** Hands-on operation of live telecom networks (RF, VoLTE, NOC). I know how critical infrastructure actually behaves and fails.
2. **Bridge:** Already applying automation and AI (RAG systems, AI agents) to operational and governance problems.
3. **Gap:** Next-gen networks (5G/6G, Open RAN, network slicing) widen the attack surface, and securing them needs AI-native defenses. That needs formal graduate depth.
4. **Vision 2030 fit:** Return to a national entity (SDAIA / NCA / STC / SITE …) to build AI-driven protection for the Kingdom's digital infrastructure.

## Elevator Pitch (EN, ~60 words)
_Draft:_ I'm a telecommunications engineer who has spent my career keeping live networks running. I've started using AI to automate operations, and I've seen how much exposure 5G creates. I'm pursuing a Master's in AI-driven network security so I can help a Saudi national entity secure and automate the networks the Kingdom's digital economy runs on.
